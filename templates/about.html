Expense Tracker

Expense Tracker is a simple personal finance web app that helps you keep track of your day-to-day spending. Instead of relying on scattered notes or memory, you can log every expense with an amount, category, date, and optional note, and instantly see where your money is going.

The goal of this project is to make personal budgeting approachable: a clear dashboard shows your totals at a glance, the expenses list lets you search and filter your history, and the statistics page turns your data into simple charts so patterns are easy to spot.

All of your data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage, so there is no account, server, or database required. Everything stays on your device.

  • 📊 Dashboard with totals and category breakdowns
  • 🔍 Search and filter your full expense history
  • ✏️ Add, edit, and delete expenses with confirmation
  • 🗂️ Category and monthly spending reports
  • 📱 Fully responsive on mobile, tablet, and desktop
  • 💾 No backend needed — powered by localStorage

Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript as a CS50 final project.
